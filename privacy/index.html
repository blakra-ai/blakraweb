Privacy

What happens to what you send us.

Who we are

BLAKRA LLC is responsible for this site and for what you send through it (the controller under the GDPR):

BLAKRA LLC
901 H St Ste 120
Sacramento, CA 95814-1817
United States

hello@blakra.us

What we collect

What you type into the contact form (why you are writing, a name or company, an email address and your message), plus the time you sent it and the page’s language, so we can reply in it. The form also checks, in your browser, how long it was open and whether a field hidden from people was filled in; these only filter out spam and are not stored. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools; its fonts and files are served from our own server. Like any web server, ours logs each request (the IP address, time, page and browser) to keep the site running and secure.

Why, and on what legal basis

To reply to you and, if you ask us to, to discuss a project, a partnership, or sponsoring or buying one of our products: the steps you ask for before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for other questions, our legitimate interest in answering them (Art. 6(1)(f)). The server logs rest on our legitimate interest in running a secure site (Art. 6(1)(f)). Using the form is voluntary; without an email address we cannot reply. We don’t sell your details, add you to a mailing list, or make automated decisions or profiles about you.

Who receives it

The form is delivered through Google Apps Script to our company inbox and to a Google Sheet that holds our record of enquiries; Google processes them on our behalf. The server logs are kept by the provider that hosts this site, also on our behalf. Nobody else receives your details unless the law requires it.

Transfers outside Europe

BLAKRA LLC is based in the United States, so what you send reaches us there, and Google stores it on its servers, which may be in the United States. Google LLC takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, including its UK and Swiss extensions. Our reply to your request also needs the transfer (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)).

How long

We keep an enquiry for up to 12 months after our last exchange, then delete it; rows in our enquiry Sheet are deleted automatically 12 months after they arrive. If an enquiry leads to a contract, it is kept with that contract’s records for as long as the law requires. Server logs are deleted after 14 days.

In your browser

If you switch motion off, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage, because you asked for it. It never leaves your device, and nothing else is stored in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, to give you a copy, or to restrict its use. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time. You can also complain to a data protection authority, in particular where you live or work.

If you live in California

In the last 12 months we collected, only as described above: identifiers (a name, an email address, an IP address), the professional or commercial details you choose to put in a message, and internet activity (the server logs). We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information. You may ask to know, correct or delete what we hold, and we will not treat you differently for asking. The site tracks no one across other sites, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If what we do with your details changes, we update this page and the date below.
