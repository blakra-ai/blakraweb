## Creative brief: "Drawn in, built up, sent out"
Need: a cinematic, story-driven scroll that proves BLAKRA's craft and leaves a business buyer thinking "a serious team; they understood my need; I would own what they build".

Ruled out as the main idea of any section (owner, inventor, strategist):
- fade-ins
- generic parallax
- split-text reveals
- horizontal carousels
- hover tilt
- particle fields
- a dark space theme
- "falls in" wording, which echoes the "money pit" meaning of a black hole

**Story (one sentence).** Your need is drawn into one centre of gravity, built up through seven layers along the spin axis, and comes back out as a system you own.

**Insight** (inference from McKinney et al. 2012 and NASA HEASARC): matter falling toward a spinning black hole is partly launched outward as jets along the spin axis. Our seven chakras already sit on that axis. So the brand's one negative reading, a sink, becomes its message: what comes in leaves stronger, through the seven layers.

**The protagonist** is the visitor's own need: a mote in the colours of the layers it takes. The visitor creates it in Pulse, and it travels the page: through Proof, up the axis, out along the jet into Contact.

## Signature moments to prototype as scroll films
1. **Hero spaghettification.** Done: `scratchpad/lab/video/blakra-hero-film.mp4`.
2. **Accretion → spectral match:** tap "Online shop", the mote condenses, then scroll to the matching case with glowing lines.
3. **Axis jet → owned key:** the mote climbs, a lotus tap shows the load path, then the key flies out to Contact with the form prefilled.

## Kill criteria (strategist)
- **Performance:** mobile Lighthouse below 85, LCP above 2.5 s, or sustained frame rate below 50 fps on a mid-range Android after one optimisation pass. Then fall back to baked stills.
- **Clarity:** of 5 business users, fewer than 4 can say what BLAKRA builds after 30 seconds, fewer than 3 notice the prefill, or anyone feels tracked.
- **Accessibility:** any flash faster than 3 per second, content reachable only through the canvas, or broken reduced-motion or keyboard paths.

## Owner questions
1. May the principals' cities or time zones appear? Default: no.
2. May the product silhouette resemble the real interface? Default: a generic outline.
3. Case layer lines use only the layers named in each case's text (default, no question needed).
