# Screenplay: "Gravity", one continuous shot
**Logline.** Every organization starts scattered. One centre of gravity pulls the pieces into order, builds them up into seven layers, and launches them as systems that run. At the end the visitor's own piece takes its place in the system.

**The arc.** Scattered → gravity → your need → built up → launched → running.

**The bookend.** The first and last shots use the same wide framing. First, loose fragments drift with nothing holding them. Last, the same space holds a calm, complete system.

The scroll is the clock: t runs from 0 to 1 over the whole home page, smoothed. Every beat has an action (something happens) and a reaction (the world answers the visitor).

## Rules
- **Motion follows gravity.** Things fall faster near the centre and slow further out, and every value is damped. Nothing jumps.
- **Text is real HTML.** It enters only as its beat begins, scrolls naturally, and is never faded to partial opacity.
- **The camera always moves for a reason:** a new subject, a reveal, or a change of scale.
- **Reduced motion:** the same story as six still frames with their text.
- **Phones:** the same film with fewer fragments and shorter holds, keeping the same beats.
