# The seven yantras, drawn true, and the layer each one stands for

Each chakra has a traditional diagram (yantra): a ring of lotus petals, an inner geometric figure for its element, and a seed syllable at the centre. We draw them exactly, as precise line geometry, the way an engineer draws a part. The petals, the figure and the proportions are the traditional ones. The seed syllables stay out of the site's text, which never shows Persian or Sanskrit script; at most they appear as a drawn glyph in the art, owner's choice.

## Drawing rules (any style)

- **Geometry:** circles, polygons and petals from a radial grid of 360/n. Petals are true lotus arcs (two circle arcs meeting at a point), never squashed spheres.
- **Line weights:**
  - Construction lines: 0.5 px, at 20% ink.
  - Petal outlines: 1 px.
  - The inner figure: 1.5 px.
- **Annotations, engineering-style:** a leader line, the layer's name in mono, the petal count as a dimension (for example "Ø 12").
- **In 3D:** the same geometry extruded or engraved into a material: glass, chrome or ink. It is the same symbol, so the line drawing and the object read as one.
- **The black hole at the centre of every yantra:** the bindu (the point at a yantra's centre) is the event horizon. This is BLAKRA's own twist, true to both sources: in tradition, the bindu is the point everything emerges from and returns to.
