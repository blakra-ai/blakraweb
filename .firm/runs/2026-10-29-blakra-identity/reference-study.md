# Reference study: landonorris.com (Awwwards Site of the Year 2025, OFF+BRAND)

The owner, 2026-10-29: "Exactly like this, but go beyond it, with very special upgrades and stranger ideas. The characters and symbols must relate to black holes, chakras, technology, space, an astronaut or a special technological world, and AI: special and excellent."

Earlier choices that still hold:
- Keep the black hole and chakra as the core idea, deeper and authentic (real yantras, real light bending).
- The scroll's purpose is to convey the brand: beauty and memorability, shorter.
- Blend four styles: sculptural luxury 3D, technical sacred geometry, cosmic realism, and real software treated as objects.

## What makes it work

It is not an effect. It is a complete identity system, held everywhere:

| Device | Lando | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Character | Lando himself: real portraits | A person to meet, not a company |
| Iconic object | His helmet; in the hero it assembles in 3D around his head | One object carries the brand |
| Signature pattern | His "blobs", on the helmet and on his clothes | Recognisable at any size |
| Signature colour | Lime, on a dark olive or an off-white paper | Owned colour |
| Handwriting | His signature, drawn in lime over the portrait | The human mark |
| Texture | Race-track contour lines behind every section | Meaning, not decoration |
| Type | A bold condensed sans with an elegant serif italic, in the accent colour | Voice: bold and refined |

## Sections, top to bottom

1. Loader: lime screen, the LN monogram drawn stroke by stroke.
2. Hero: the portrait, with the 3D helmet assembling around the head (wireframe crown, visor strip, chin guard); contour lines; a small "next race" widget.
3. A huge marquee statement over a portrait, with the signature scrawled across it.
4. Manifesto in huge mixed type ("REDEFINING LIMITS, FIGHTING FOR WINS…").
5. A floating collage of photos, each with a small mono caption, with parallax.
6. A split, On track / Off track: helmet on one side, profile on the other, a handwritten word over it, two square accent buttons.
7. Helmets: a full-bleed photo, then a gallery grid of every helmet design (a collection of variations of the one object).
8. Store promotion.
9. Socials: a fan of cards.
10. Footer.

## The lesson for BLAKRA

Build the same complete system with our own meanings, then go beyond it:

- **Character:** an astronaut or navigator, and/or the three principals.
- **Iconic object:** a helmet whose visor holds the black hole.
- **Signature pattern:** from the chakra yantras or the accretion disk.
- **Signature colour:** to be decided.
- **Human mark:** an orbit or light trail drawn like a signature.
- **Texture:** the contour lines of a gravity well that bend around the cursor (a mass).
- **Type:** Geist and Newsreader italic already pair the same way.
