# Art direction: BLAKRA — "The Navigator"

**Brief (owner, 2026-10-29):** exactly like landonorris.com, but beyond it: special upgrades and stranger ideas, with a character and symbols from black holes, chakras, technology, space, astronauts and AI.

**Already decided:**
- Keep the core idea and make it authentic (real yantras, real lensing).
- The scroll conveys the brand: short and memorable.
- Blend sculptural luxury, technical sacred geometry, cosmic realism and real software.

Inputs: reference-study.md, yantras.md, and the inventor's three finalists (Seven Systems, Calibre 7, The Hole That Builds). This direction merges the first and third, and takes one idea from the second.

## The system (Lando's devices, BLAKRA's meanings)

| Device | BLAKRA |
|---|---|
| Character | **The Navigator**: BLAKRA's astronaut, at the rim of the black hole, lit from behind by its Einstein ring |
| Iconic object | **The helmet**. Its visor holds the black hole (a real lensed starfield and the seven-colour disk). The seven yantras are engraved round its rim. The antenna is the crown (AI); the side lamps are the third eye. |
| Signature reveal (Lando's portrait ↔ helmet) | **The X-ray cursor**: under the cursor the helmet and suit turn into their engineering blueprint, seven subsystems drawn as the true yantras, each labelled with its software layer |
| Signature pattern | The seven yantras in fine line work: on the helmet, on seals, on buttons, in the loader |
| Texture (Lando's track contours) | **Gravity-well contour lines** behind every section. They bend round the cursor, which is a small mass. |
| Colour | Near-black space and off-white blueprint paper, alternating. One owned accent: infrared orange (#ff5a1f, already the near side of the mark's disk), plus the chakra spectrum used only inside symbols. |
| Human mark (Lando's signature) | The Navigator's light trail: an orbit drawn in one stroke, as a signature |
| Type | A bold condensed sans with Newsreader italic in the accent (the same pairing Lando uses). |

The seven subsystems map one-to-one. Each layer keeps its chakra and its yantra:

| Chakra (layer) | Subsystem |
|---|---|
| Root (cloud and backend) | Life-support pack |
| Sacral (data) | Cooling-water loop |
| Solar plexus (automation) | Chest control module |
| Heart (design) | Pressure layer |
| Throat (web and mobile) | Comms cap |
| Third eye (launch and growth) | Visor lamps |
| Crown (AI) | Antenna |

## Sections (each one device, each with a purpose)

1. **Loader:** the mark drawn as one orbit stroke; the gravity lines settle.
2. **Hero:**
   - The Navigator at the rim of the hole. The helmet assembles as the page opens (as Lando's helmet does round his head).
   - The X-ray cursor reveals the blueprint underneath.
   - Headline in huge type, "We build what organizations run on."
3. **Manifesto:** huge mixed type ("SOFTWARE WITH GRAVITY"); the light-trail signature is drawn across it.
4. **The Hole That Builds** (our work in one gesture):
   - A real messy input (an order list in an email, a spreadsheet) falls into the hole, and a clean, working interface comes out of the jet.
   - The cards are real HTML.
5. **Mission patches** (Lando's helmet wall):
   - One embroidered-style patch per real project: Retail, Logistics, Insurance, Manufacturing, E-commerce.
   - Each is generated from the project itself: its layers' colours, its yantras, a guilloché seal that can't be forged.
   - Click a patch for the case: before, now, outcomes.
6. **Client work / Our products** (Lando's On track / Off track): a split. The payload, the skin-analysis AI, sits in a sealed capsule.
7. **The crew** (Lando's socials fan): the three principals as crew badges, with real names and roles.
8. **Reply signal** (contact):
   - The strange idea, kept honest: the Navigator is falling toward the horizon, so his beacons reach us slower and redder.
   - Your message is the reply signal. On sending, it rides the jet out.

The film is shorter: about 8 to 10 screens, against about 20 today.

## Assets

- **The full astronaut body** needs a modelled character: a commissioned or commercially licensed model in glTF. The owner decides; the site never downloads one on its own.
- **Until then:** the hero leads with the helmet, which can be built in code (lathe and shell geometry, visor shader, engraved yantras), with the body as a dark silhouette rim-lit by the ring.
- **Built in code:** the lensed black hole, the blueprints, the patches and seals, the gravity lines and the light trail.
