## Rules (with the reason for each)
1. **Scale changes only through a "cut on action" or a camera move.** Cut on action: she steps out of the frame in one shot and enters the next shot in the same pose and direction. The eye follows the motion and accepts the new scale, as editors do. With no cut, a scale change must take at least 600 px of scroll.
2. **She always stands on something.** That can be a grid line, the rim of a band, a row's baseline or the rim of the ring. Standing on something is what makes gravity readable.
3. **Each move has a target.** She moves toward the next item, looks at it, and only then does her action.
4. **Rule of thirds and lead room.** Her eyes sit on the upper third line. Space is left in front of her face, toward the text she is looking at. She never sits at the centre of a disc, because a centred figure reads as a logo.
5. **Text wins.** Where text is dense she leaves the frame on purpose, with an exit that points to the next thing.
6. **Gravity grows with depth.** Stride frequency drops from 1.2 to 0.9 Hz. From scene 08 her ears are pulled back toward the hole. Then the grid's pull grows too.
