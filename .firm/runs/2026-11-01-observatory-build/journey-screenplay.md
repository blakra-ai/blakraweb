# The Navigator's descent: screenplay (2026-11-02)

Story in one line: the Navigator deer leads the visitor down into the black hole; at every station she does one thing with a reason, and gravity grows the deeper they go.

## Three fixed rules
1. **Size changes only through the camera.** She is one physical size in the world. Bigger or smaller means the camera moved closer or further, always continuously and never in a jump.
2. **One continuous camera per scene.** On the paper sections it tracks her side-on, like a documentary. In the dark windows it pushes in slowly.
3. **Gravity grows with depth.** The grid's pull, the pull on her ears and the slowing of her steps all increase from 01 to 09. At 09 she reaches the edge of the hole.

## Scenes
| # | Section | Her action (purpose) | Camera | Gravity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hero | She stands at the edge of the hole facing the visitor. On the first scroll she turns and takes her first step toward the descent. **Invitation.** | Close-up, low angle, slow push-in | 0.1 |
| 02 | Why one team | She walks an unbroken orange line. Wherever the line could break she keeps going. **One crew, no hand-offs.** | Side-on, medium, tracking | 0.2 |
| 03 | Seven layers | She climbs seven bands like steps, from root to crown. The band under her feet lights. **Building from the foundation up.** | Camera tilts up with the climb | 0.3 |
| 04 | What do you need built? | She stops and looks at the visitor, ears turned to the cursor. When the visitor chooses, she nods and moves on. **She listens.** | Close, holds still | 0.35 |
| 05 | Proof | She walks past five cases and stops at the one nearest the visitor's choice. She lowers her head and her eyes scan it, and the orange stitch appears. **Finds the evidence.** | Side tracking, pauses on that case | 0.5 |
| 06 | How we work | Three deliberate steps, and each step lights one stage. **Step by step.** | Medium, three short dolly moves | 0.6 |
| 07 | Products | She reaches the sealed well and sits beside it without entering. **Confidential, protected.** | Medium, still | 0.7 |
| 08 | People | She steps back so the three names are seen. **Here people are the point, not her.** | Wide, she is small at the edge of the frame | 0.8 |
| 09 | Contact | The grid pulls hard and her steps get heavy. She reaches the dark window, looks back once at the visitor, then waits beside the form at the edge of the ring. On "Send" she walks into the hole. **The end of the descent.** | Camera follows behind her, then a slow push-in | 1.0 |

## Timing
- Each scene is one screen of scrolling. Walking happens only between scenes. At the station itself she stops and does her action.
- Reduced motion: the final frame of each scene, still.
- Phones: only scenes 01 and 09, in the dark windows.
