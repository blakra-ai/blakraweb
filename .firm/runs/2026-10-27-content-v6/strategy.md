# blakra.us positioning and structure brief (content v6, 2026-10-27)

Labels: **[F]** fact (owner answers or FACTS.md) · **[I]** inference · **[OWNER]** needs the owner before it ships.

## 1. Positioning

**The idea to own: "Built to run."** BLAKRA builds the systems an organization runs on every day (operations, data, commerce, assistants), and it builds them to work in production, on the client's own data, owned by the client. Every confirmed fact points this way. The five case studies are operational systems [F]. The owner's list is back-office and AI-on-private-data work: admin panels, shops, warehouse and databases, bots, and an AI on one organization's security-sensitive data [F]. "Speed and precision" [F] is how they work, not what they sell.

**Two-sentence pitch (copywriter: use as written, or tighten it):**
> BLAKRA builds the software organizations run on: operations and inventory systems, admin panels and shops, apps, bots, and AI that works on their own sensitive data. Three principals lead every project from the first scope to production, and we put the same engineering into products of our own.

**Why this beats "we build everything" [I]:** a visitor will notice three things: a company founded in 2024, three named people, and no client names. Put next to those, "everything" reads as the claim a weak shop makes, and it cannot be checked. Trust comes from facts that can be checked: a clear category, proof in production, named people, written terms, and stated limits (the "Not a fit" line already does this well). The breadth stays visible as a list of what was delivered, as evidence. It is not the headline.

**Lead with client services. The frame holds both businesses: "an engineering company that builds for clients and builds its own products."** Goals 1 and 2 are about the same visitor and the same trust. Goal 3 is a small, deliberate audience that will follow one clear link. On the home page, products serve as proof of capability ("we ship whole products at our own risk") and lead to a dedicated page.

## 4. The metaphor

**Where it helps:**
- the name's origin, said once;
- memorability through the visuals;
- the root-to-crown order, which is a real argument (AI sits on data and infrastructure);
- the event horizon at Contact;
- the 404 page.

**Where it hurts:** wherever a buyer has to decode it to get the facts. Examples: the H1 "gravity", the orbit note, "Map the weight", "Same engine, aimed outward", "beneath it is open", the chakra labels. To a corporate buyer, chakra vocabulary in body copy reads as esoteric [I], and the owner has already rejected copy as "unclear" [F].

**Rule:** the visuals carry the metaphor. At most one short caption per section; every heading, lede and label is plain engineering language.

## 5. What is missing (truthful items only)

Ready now [F]: ownership of code and data; fixed scope and price for the first system; working software every two weeks; free assessment; 24-hour reply; "Not a fit".

Each item that needs the owner is a question with a recommended default:
1. **Confidentiality and references.** "Client names are kept confidential; we sign an NDA before you share details. References by call where a client agrees." *Default: publish the NDA line; omit references until two clients agree (FACTS).*
2. **Security and data handling.** Can we say: access only to what the project needs, client data never used to train models, AI can be deployed on the client's own infrastructure without third-party AI services? *Default: publish only the first two after confirmation.*
3. **Skin AI.** Does it run without any external model API? *Default: say only "written from scratch, no third-party dependencies".*
4. **Legal entity and jurisdiction.** It goes in the footer and Privacy (the US compliance title implies a US entity). *Default: required before launch.*
5. **Engagement shapes and support after launch.** *Default: show "fixed-scope project" plus "ongoing support by agreement".*
6. **Working languages and time zones.** The site is in de/fr/es. *Default: "We work in English", plus any other language confirmed.*
7. **Product facts.** For each product: name, one line, status, terms on offer. *Default: skin AI only.*
8. **Specialists joining.** *Default: footer line "Engineers and designers: hello@blakra.us".*

**New insight [I]:** two facts the owner cannot publish by name fit together: an AI built on one organization's security-sensitive data, and a skin AI with no third-party dependencies. Together they support a claim most AI vendors cannot make: *AI that does not need to send your data to someone else's model.* That is the edge that serves both the client goal and the product goal. It ships only if question 3 confirms it.

Prior-art note: guidance on publishing work under NDA agrees that anonymized cases are credible only when the anonymization is explained and the role and detail are specific (fueler.io, vantagepoint.io, retrieved 2026-10-27).
