# Storyboard revision 1 — consolidated from the pre-mortem and the UX review (2026-11-01)

Blockers
1. The guide must be consequential and real: one rendered still of the Astraía bust lit at the rim (needs the owner's download); on the frames give her one act per station where she appears (06: she lights the layers the picker chose; 04: she turns to the stitched row). Fallback is Cyber Girl, never the ring alone.
2. Story order: the picker ("What do you need built?") closes the held stage (03b) so proof (04) can stitch the nearest case honestly; "Where we've done it" then points down, never up.
3. Hero: perform the system's own device on load: the straight grid bends into the well over 900 ms (the only thing that moves), the hole grows station by station; the split layout alone is "seen before".
4. No text through opacity: unlit layer labels in solid grey (5.49:1), lit ones porcelain; lit state by dot and weight.
5. Windows grow with content: min-height 100svh, height auto; the canvas scissor follows; nothing clipped at 200 % zoom, 320×568 or German copy.
6. Form states drawn: inline errors with aria-describedby and a focused summary; sending (aria-busy); success as a static role=status with focus; honeypot hidden properly; network failure keeps the text.

Major
7. Accent discipline: gold ≤ 3 DOM elements per frame (ring glow, armillary rings, caret, marker, Send); the chakra dots appear only where layers are the subject (stage, picker, proof rows), not on every screen.
8. Porcelain screen gets one large object (the ring glyph in ink with the picked layers lit, or the numeral row); products and people move to the proof's hairline-row style; it must not read as "the 3D ran out".
9. The 02→04 hand-over (ring shrinks into the marker) is scrubbed over the hold's last 10 %, not timed.
10. Field and door borders ≥ 3:1; selected door marked by a check, not colour alone; focus ring on porcelain = ink ring + porcelain gap; scroll-padding-top ≈ 88 px.
11. Lenis: native scroll, none under reduced motion; typed lines are full text with a clip reveal and a static caret under reduced motion.
12. Phone: no rotated gauge text; a 2 px progress rule or a "04 / 09" chip in the nav; consistent 24 px inset; compact "Start a project" in the nav from 360 px; the Menu panel designed; words on the stage in an opaque band.
13. Tablet 768–1100 defined (hamburger, her line stacked under the ring); 1920 max-width ≈ 1600 with clamp() type; mono floor 12 px, shorten labels instead of shrinking.
14. Layer dots get a text equivalent ("Uses layers 2, 4, 5, 7"), dots aria-hidden; "Where we've done it" wraps; the picker never overwrites typed text and announces.
15. Copy: "orange stitch" → a gold word in four locales (owner's call); language control becomes a four-option menu; motion toggle = button with aria-pressed; heading order h2/h3 explicit.

Keeps (confirmed by both reviews): native scroll, no pins; the same object seen through the next window; all text in DOM; the contact screen; honest facts; the phone path without WebGL.
