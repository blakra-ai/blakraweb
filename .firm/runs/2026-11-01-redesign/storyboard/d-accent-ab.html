Recommendation · gold
Gold is the accretion light the ring already emits, and it is the brass of her armillary rings: one accent, one material, one object. It passes AA as text with 2.4 points to spare, so mono readouts and the eyebrow may carry it on both dark surfaces. It separates us from the orange-on-black register (basement, Darkmoon) the jury has scored before.
What orange keeps
Continuity with the OG image and the current marker, and one line of the Navigator's copy ("wears the orange stitch"). Against it: 6.31 : 1 is the floor, orange on near-black is the most common dark-site accent of the set, and beside brass rings it reads as two warm hues. If gold is chosen, that one word of copy needs the owner's change in four locales; the OG image is regenerated by `npm run og`.
