Motion grammar

  • Moves: the thread (draws on, 1 beat per chapter), the probe along it (eye follows the cursor), the grid lines nearest the probe (warp), the seven bands (light on choice).
  • Never: text, backgrounds, text opacity; the warp never crosses a text box.
  • Hand-over: at an ink chapter the grid inverts and the thread turns white; the probe stays orange.
  • Hover: the probe turns its eye to the hovered item; its grid line bends 6 px toward it.
  • Click: the thread re-routes through the chosen need; the matching bands light.
  • Reduced motion: thread fully drawn, grid warped statically, probe still.
