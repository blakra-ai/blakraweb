Motion grammar

  • Moves: display words within her radius (Geist wght 300→600, rotate ≤ 2.5°), the card row (into a lensed arc), her gaze, blink, breath and hair, the thin ring when she is off-screen.
  • Never: body text, backgrounds, text opacity, the lede, the form.
  • Hand-over: the ring crosses the paper/ink hairline and inverts.
  • Hover: the cursor is a second, weaker mass: the nearest display word bows 2 weight units.
  • Click: the chosen need lights its bands in her irises, root to crown.
  • Reduced motion: HTML is the final state: words straight, bands lit, she is a still frame.
