## 2. Recurring patterns, 2025-26 winners

All from Awwwards pages above, checked 2026-11-01.
- **Two-colour palettes dominate.** Nearly every page above lists exactly two colours; many are near-black plus one hot accent (#FF8539, #D2FF00, #F15534, #01C654).
- **Story is the spine, product is the proof.** Why Zero, Terminal Industries, Heron AI, Oryzo all tag Storytelling and are product/startup sites.
- **One object or one world, not many effects.** Oryzo (coaster), Messenger (planet), Darkmoon (moon) use one scene.
- **Two valid stacks.** WebGL/Three.js (Oryzo, Why Zero, MECHA) and Webflow + GSAP (Heron, Lando, Neutomni, Signal-A). Terminal Industries won on CSS/Vue alone.
- **Interaction is a verb.** "Hold to proceed", gestures, game controls, hotspot hovers, rather than scroll-only.
- **Motion is scored separately.** Dev Award (SOTD winners, over 7) rewards Animations/Transitions (8.2-9.6 typical), WPO (7-8.8). Accessibility is the weakest sub-score in almost every page (6.0-7.6). Weights: Design 40, Usability 30, Creativity 20, Content 10 (https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/).
- **Sound appears** as a tag on Why Zero, Santioni, Zentry.
- Secondary (search snippets only, not opened): trend roundups agree on oversized type, scroll-driven motion, `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks (https://line25.com/articles/web-design-trends-2026/, https://reallygooddesigns.com/web-design-trends-2026/).

## 3. Anti-patterns

Evidence here is thinner; mark as judgment.
- Parallax for its own sake: MECHA is the only parallax-tagged recent win and has a low SOTD (7.28). Secondary sources warn of mobile jank and Core Web Vitals (line25 above, snippet only).
- Ignoring accessibility: Star Atlas 5.8, GQ & AP 6.0.
- Heavy 3D on a content site: Star Atlas WPO 6.4. Winners hold WPO at 8+ (Oryzo 8.6, Terminal 8.2, Messenger 8.8).
- Game-first sites (Bruno's Portfolio, Awards Racing, Dont Look Up) win on craft but are not a product narrative.
- "AI purple" and generic gradients: UNCONFIRMED as a jury penalty; Navigate uses #8584FF, Raycast a blue gradient. Judgment only.
- Pinned sections that trap scroll: UNCONFIRMED from sources read; I could not play any site.

## 4. Five closest to guide + space/physics + software

1. **Messenger** (abeto, SOTY 2025). Right: character, NPCs, small planet, Dev 8.21, WPO 8.8. Wrong or risky: it is a game, no product information; fonts and a11y not verifiable.
2. **Why Zero** (SOTM Sep 2026). Right: scroll narrative plus hold-to-proceed plus map for a tech product. Wrong: a11y and semantics 6.6; green-on-white is not "gravity".
3. **Navigate** (Resn, SOTD Apr 2025). Right: character animation with data viz for a platform. Wrong: a11y 6.6; crypto/Web3 positioning.
4. **Darkmoon** (nominee). Right: a shader moon you glide over, with a flat black dashboard so the real UI stays legible. Wrong: not yet judged; security niche.
5. **Star Atlas** (Hello Monday, 2021). Right: full space-narrative precedent. Wrong: dated, WPO 6.4, a11y 5.8, game marketing.

Honest gap: I found no 2024-26 Awwwards winner that has a black hole, a guide and a software company together. The combination is open ground.
