## 6. FAQ (/about)

**Who owns the code and the data?** You do, from day one. Everything is built on standard, documented tools, so it stays fully yours to run and extend.

**Do you sign an NDA?** Yes, as the first step, before you share any details.

**Who will we work with?** A named account lead, a team staffed from our practices for your project, and our leadership team, hands-on throughout. Everyone works under one BLAKRA contract.

**How do you work across time zones?** At kickoff we fix a daily overlap with your working hours, and we keep it for the whole project. **[DEFAULT]**

**What language do projects run in?** English, or another language agreed at kickoff. **[DEFAULT]**

**How do you protect our data?** An NDA comes first. Your code and data stay yours. Access is limited to the people who need it for their part of the work. When the data is sensitive, we run the system, open-source models included, on your own infrastructure. **[CONFIRM]** the least-privilege access line describes current practice.

**What happens after launch?** We monitor, fix and improve the system with your team under a written support agreement.

**What kind of work do you take on?** Software that runs a real process: operations, data, apps and AI. For brochure websites, a web designer is the better choice.

**How do we start?** Describe the work. Within 24 hours, an engineer who would build it replies with what it involves, the risks we see and a clear first step.
