Netguru empowers leading brands to transform B2B solutions, marketplaces, and retail ecosystems with cutting-edge technology, AI-powered personalization, and scalability. Since 2008, it has supported forward-thinking businesses in driving growth across the US, UK, DACH, and MENA regions. A certified B Corporation®, Netguru is trusted by major brands including IKEA, VW, OLX, Żabka, Careem, and Vinted.
