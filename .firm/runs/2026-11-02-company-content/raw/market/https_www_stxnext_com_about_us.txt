OUR AI PHILOSOPHY
A Reasonable
‍AI Approach


Four principles that keep AI spend accountable to outcomes, in the order we apply them on every engagement.

EXPLORE AI SERVICES
MINDSET
Honest & Grounded Advisory

We scrutinize project briefs to protect your budget and industry regulations. If a mandate asks for AI where standard deterministic software or automation tools work better or safer, we explain the trade-offs and recommend the best fit. We'd rather lose the AI scope than deliver something you regret in six months.

BASELINE
Value-First Implementation

We embed AI strictly inside workflows where automation drives direct financial returns, lowering operating costs and/or accelerating execution. During discovery, we run custom ROI calculators and create prototypes against your real data to forecast value and ensure we understand (and challenge) your end goal before you commit a budget. 

IMPLEMENTATION
Infrastructure Before Intelligence

We refactor flawed data and cloud architecture at the foundation level. Preparing your data layer ensures AI workloads process queries with high model accuracy, real-time context and grounding. Optimized, modern cloud layer delivers elastic compute and cost-efficient infrastructure required to scale workload seamlessly across your organization. If your industry regulations or company prefers it, we work with Sovereign Cloud partners and with on-premise deployments as well.

TEAM MODEL
Direct Senior Ownership

Instead of being handed off from one team to another and changing contact points through every stage, one senior engineer stays with your system from day one of our conversations through deployment. 

‍

They start by scrutinizing your operational pain to ensure we only build what matters. Supported by targeted domain experts and our Agentic Engineering / AI-native SDLC framework which speeds up development by 2-10x (2x-5x for brownfield, 10x+ for greenfield), a tight AI Pod with an FDE outpaces a traditional team of ten.

OUR HERITAGE
Build on Python Excellence

Python has become the undisputed lingua franca of modern artificial intelligence, especially for AI Agents. At STX Next, we have been building, scaling, and optimizing in Python for over two decades now.

That long-term focus means your AI initiatives are engineered by teams who know the language inside and out, delivering secure, scalable software from day-to-day model integration to enterprise data pipelines.

Our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

The success of our customers and co-workers depends on smooth processes and informed decision-making. We have a roster of seasoned leaders applying the best management and communication practices on the market to our work.

We hold the EcoVadis Bronze Medal, placing us in the top 35% of sustainable companies globally. It validates our commitment to green energy and responsible operations.

Environmental Sustainability

From 2024, we are using 100% green energy to power our Poznań office.

We organized a Sports Challenge with points translating into 300 trees planted in Żabno near Poznań.

Since early 2025, our welcome packs have featured eco-friendly, top-quality products designed to last for years—and we plant a tree for every new team member.

Engaged Team

We provided many D&I initiatives, promoting a stronger and more innovative team.

Our Growth Review process and transparent salary grading ensure tailored development plans and clear paths to career growth and salary raises.

Each year, we come together to support the Szlachetna Paczka initiative, helping families living in poverty.

Social Responsibility

We organize open PAIDA and DevDuck knowledge sharing events to support local communities.

We extend our global reach through aid and support for Ukraine, including fundraising, equipment donations and housing assistance.
