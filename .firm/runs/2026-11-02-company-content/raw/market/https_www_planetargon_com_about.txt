About Us
We help teams get more out of the software they already have. Whether you’re trying to reduce risk, move faster, or finally tackle the technical debt that’s been quietly compounding... we’ve been doing this for over two decades.

Planet Argon was founded in Portland more than 20 years ago, and our core team still calls it home. Around them is a distributed group of engineers across North America, South America, and Europe. Together, we support product teams through every phase of software delivery. That includes firefighting, planning, cleanups, and long-term improvement.

We’re not a big agency. That’s intentional. We hire carefully and invest in people who are thoughtful, dependable, and genuinely enjoy collaborative, client-facing work. The result is a small, nimble team with strong technical judgment and a people-first mindset.

We believe software should be delivered with care. Agile is more than a process. It’s a posture. And we believe that a great partnership should leave your team better than it was the day before. More confident. More capable. Better prepared for what comes next.

If you’re looking for a steady partner who helps you move forward with clarity, you’re in the right place.

Our Core Values
PROACTIVE

We actively seek opportunities to improve our client's products, our processes, and our abilities.

CURIOUS

A natural curiosity for the undiscovered results in remarkable work for our clients – and stronger connections for our team. We ask questions, experiment, and learn.

DEPENDABLE

We are invested in our work. We manage expectations. We support our clients and teammates. We hold ourselves, our teammates, and our clients accountable.

DELIGHTFUL

We choose to set a mindful, positive tone that allows everyone to flourish.

VERSATILE

We readily adapt to change and encourage innovation because our team and work are transparent and flexible.

On Rails Podcast

The On Rails Podcast is a new project from the Rails Foundation, hosted by Planet Argon's CEO, Robby Russell. It's not just another dev podcast—it's a space for thoughtful conversations with the people who maintain and evolve real-world Rails applications. Each episode shares the lessons, challenges, and decisions behind keeping software resilient over time. If you care about sustainability, maintainability, and clarity in your Rails codebase, this podcast was made for you!
