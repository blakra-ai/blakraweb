The body copy was already disciplined (plain declaratives, sourced numbers, no hype); most of it was left alone deliberately. Changes:

1. **Guide lines rewritten in all locales.** The mascot voice ("I'm the Navigator… look into my eyes", "missions flown", "payload", "crew", "gold stitch", "my seven systems") read as childish and also renamed the seven *layers* as "systems", clashing with the case studies' "systems". New lines are impersonal, purposeful, and use "layers" consistently (e.g. hero: "Each stage of this page answers one question about your project."). All slots stay filled.
2. **SEO.** All meta descriptions were 220–260 chars; now 149–155. de/fr/es home titles were 63–72 chars; now ≤59. Privacy title became "Privacy policy"; title dashes unified to " — ". 404 title now "Page not found — BLAKRA" in en.
3. **404** "This page fell past the horizon" → "Page not found." with a useful explanation and "Back to the home page".
4. **Unclear lines fixed:** pulse "For clients under NDA: see Also delivered." → "Delivered for clients under NDA. See Also delivered."; form error "Tell us a little more: at least a sentence." → "Please add a little more detail: one sentence at least."; the (unused) cursor hint no longer genders the figure.
5. Kept exactly: 24-hour reply, all case numbers, every fact in FACTS.md. No new claims.
