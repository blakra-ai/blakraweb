THE PROBLEM

Manual inspection at line speed is inconsistent, and the only record of it is a paper sheet. A missed defect is then found by the customer, after the batch has been packed and shipped.

THE DESIGN
01

Four camera feeds are shown together, each labelled with its camera number and frame rate.

02

A detection box stays attached to the frame it belongs to and names the defect class and the confidence.

03

The summary row states inspected count, pass rate and defects for the current shift.

04

A batch report keeps the annotated image next to each recorded defect, so a call can be reviewed later.

05

States use shape as well as colour, because the screen is read in low light and at a glance.

NOTE

Concept work. Screens are design mockups, not a client deployment.
