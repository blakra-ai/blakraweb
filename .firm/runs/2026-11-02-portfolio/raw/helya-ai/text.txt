CONCEPT STUDY · INTERNAL KNOWLEDGE ASSISTANT
Helya

An assistant that answers from an organisation’s own documents and shows where the answer came from.

Designed for teams whose procedures live in long documents. It answers in prose and cites the document and page behind each part of the answer.

What is the approval process for equipment purchases over $10,000?
Here’s the process outlined in your procurement policy.

Equipment purchases above $10,000 require two levels of approval before a purchase order can be issued.

Submit a purchase request. Include the vendor quote, cost centre, and business justification. 1
Department head approval. Your department head reviews the request and confirms budget availability. 1
Finance sign-off. Finance validates the spend and releases the purchase order. 2
Typical turnaround: 2–3 business days after submission.

THE PROBLEM

Policies and maintenance notes are written for completeness, then stored in shared drives. Finding the current clause takes longer than the task it describes, so people ask a colleague instead.

THE DESIGN
01

Answers cite the document and page beside each claim, and the source passage is shown in a side panel.

02

Document access follows the reader’s permissions rather than the index, so a restricted file stays restricted.

03

Unanswered questions are collected as gaps, which is the only reliable signal of what the index is missing.

04

The answer column stays narrow, because the content is prose rather than tables.

05

The composer holds its position at the bottom instead of moving between turns.

NOTE

Concept work. Screens are design mockups, not a client deployment.
