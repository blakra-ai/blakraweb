THE PROBLEM

A shop that sells in two places usually records sales in one tool and stock in another. Size availability lags behind the shop floor, and a delivery is counted on paper before anyone types it in.

THE DESIGN
01

Product screens read availability from the record the staff app writes, so the two views cannot disagree.

02

The stockroom screen opens on a scan field; a barcode returns the item, the size and the current count.

03

Low-stock items are listed above full inventory and ordered by how quickly they sell.

04

Stock receipts are entered per size rather than as one total, which keeps the count usable after the delivery.

05

Staff controls sit in a separate mode, so a customer holding the phone cannot open them.

NOTE

Concept work. Screens are design mockups, not a client deployment.
