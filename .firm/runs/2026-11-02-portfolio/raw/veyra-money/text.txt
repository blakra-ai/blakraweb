Veyra

A money app built around what can be spent now.

THE PROBLEM

Bank apps list transactions but rarely say what they add up to. A balance that still includes bills due looks available when it is not, and the gap is only noticed after a payment fails.

THE DESIGN
01

The home screen states the balance first, then the change over the month, then the activity behind both.

02

Spending is grouped by merchant category and set against the previous month.

03

The transfer screen keeps recipient, amount and fee visible at once, with a keypad sized for one hand.

04

Card controls sit below the card itself rather than in a settings area.

05

A dark field with a single accent colour keeps amounts legible at small sizes.

NOTE

Concept work. Screens are design mockups, not a client deployment.
