THE PROBLEM

An approval that lives in email loses its state. Nobody can say which request is waiting on whom without reading a thread, and a request that stalls leaves no trace of where it stopped.

THE DESIGN
01

Steps are placed on a canvas in the order they run, with a connector between each.

02

A condition step states its field, operator and value in a properties panel beside the canvas.

03

Approval steps name the person or role responsible, so a waiting request has an owner.

04

Run history records each step with a timestamp and a result.

05

A failed run is retried from the step that failed, not from the start.

NOTE

Concept work. Screens are design mockups, not a client deployment.
