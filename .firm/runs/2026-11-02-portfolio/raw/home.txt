HOW WE WORK
Small, senior, and visible.

Three people carry the project. There is no account layer between you and the person writing the code, and no one learning your product on your budget.

01
Frame the product

We start with the users, the constraints and the smallest version worth building. You leave with a scope and a phased plan, and a price for the first phase.

02
Design the system

Flows, a prototype and a component library. Screens are checked against real content and empty states before production code is written.

03
Build in weekly releases

A working build on a staging link each week. You can click through what changed and raise it in the same week it was built.

04
Ship and stay

Store submission, analytics and monitoring. Support after release is agreed per project rather than assumed.

THE STUDIO
Three people, registered in 2024.

Noyan is a designer and two engineers. We registered the studio in 2024; before that we worked together without a formal registration. When a project needs more hands, we hire on a temporary basis for a defined scope and close that access at handover.
