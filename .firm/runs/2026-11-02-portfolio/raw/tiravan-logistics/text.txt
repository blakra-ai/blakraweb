CONCEPT STUDY · DELIVERY DISPATCH AND DRIVER APP
Tiravan

A dispatch screen and a driver app that share one view of the day.

Designed for freight operators who currently coordinate drivers by phone. The dispatch screen shows route progress; the driver app shows the next stop and keeps it when the signal goes.

THE PROBLEM

A dispatcher who cannot see stop progress calls the driver to ask. A driver who loses signal loses the stop list with it, and proof of delivery becomes a later phone call.

THE DESIGN
01

The map marks completed and remaining stops differently on each route.

02

The route list states stops done against stops assigned, per driver.

03

The driver app keeps the stop list on the device, so a lost connection does not lose the day.

04

Proof of delivery is captured as a photo with a timestamp at the stop.

05

Arrival times are estimated from completed stops rather than from a fixed average.

NOTE

Concept work. Screens are design mockups, not a client deployment.
