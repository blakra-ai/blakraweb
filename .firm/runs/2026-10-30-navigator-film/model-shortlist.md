# Model shortlist (lead, 2026-11-01)

Where to look, in order of yield for this concept (a calm porcelain/white android guide with a face):
1. **Sketchfab** with filters Downloadable + licence CC0/CC-BY/CC-BY-SA, 15k–150k faces. The public API
   makes this a query: `https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/search?type=models&q=<terms>&downloadable=true&licenses=cc0&licenses=by&licenses=by-sa&max_face_count=150000&sort_by=-likeCount`.
   CC-BY means one line of credit on the site (footer or /legal), which is fine.
2. **Fab** (Epic's marketplace; free tier and monthly free assets; standard licence allows commercial use).
3. **Meshy / Tripo / Rodin** for a made-to-order model (paid tier for commercial rights).
4. Avoid: fan-art of licensed characters (2B, Miku, Lucy, Motoko), "from Character Creator" uploads
   (Reallusion base meshes may not be redistributable), anything without a stated licence.
