About

One company, from the first scope to the finished product.

BLAKRA LLC was founded in 2024, with its registered office in Sacramento, California. Our teams work remotely.

The name joins black hole and chakra. We build in seven layers on one axis, from cloud and backend at the root to AI at the crown, and each layer rests on the one beneath it.

BLAKRA is a software company organized in five practices: Engineering, Product Design, Data & AI, Cloud & DevOps, and Delivery. Our leadership team scopes and leads every project, and staffs it from a growing network of specialists, so each part of the work has the right expert. One contract and one accountable team carry it from the first scope to the finished product and its support after launch.

Alongside client work across a growing range of industries, we build products of our own; LIOXSA is the first.

05 / 06

Questions buyers ask

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. Everything is built on standard, documented tools, so it stays fully yours to run and extend.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, as the first step, before you share any details.

Who will we work with?

A named account lead, a team staffed from our practices for your project, and our leadership team, hands-on throughout. Everyone works under one BLAKRA contract.

How do you work across time zones?

At kickoff we fix a daily overlap with your working hours, and we keep it for the whole project.

What language do projects run in?

English, or another language agreed at kickoff.

How do you protect our data?

An NDA comes first. Your code and data stay yours. Access is limited to the people who need it for their part of the work. When the data is sensitive, we run the system, open-source models included, on your own infrastructure.

What happens after launch?

We monitor, fix and improve the system with your team under a written support agreement.

What kind of work do you take on?

Software that runs a real process: operations, data, apps and AI, from a first system to all seven layers.

How do we start?

Describe the work. Within 24 hours, an engineer who would build it replies with what it involves, the risks we see and a clear first step.
